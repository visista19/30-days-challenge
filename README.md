# 30-days-challenge
# A 30-day commitment to building consistency, learning in public, and shipping small wins every day.

## 🎯 Goal & Focus
* **Challenge Theme:** building projects, and keeping track of all the information I consumed. 
* **Daily Time Commitment:** 2 hours or 3 hours

## 📋 Rules of Engagement
1. **Show up daily:** Even a small step counts on busy days—keep the streak alive.
2. **Document progress:** Log what was built, learned, or practiced each day.
3. **Stay adaptable:** Adjust scope if needed without abandoning the 30-day cadence.
